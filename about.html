About

Bio • Focus • Links.

Hi, I’m JP 👋

I’m a cyber security student and the creator of CyberFriday. I landed my first role in cyber security about five months ago, and since then I’ve been focused on one thing: getting better every day.

CyberFriday is where I document that process — the labs I complete, the challenges I work through, the tools I experiment with, and the lessons I learn along the way.

I learn best by doing. Whether it’s a CTF, a hands-on lab, or building a small project, I break things down step by step and turn the experience into structured writeups — not just the solution, but the thinking behind it.

As of today, I’m CompTIA Security+ and TryHackMe SEC1 certified. I’m early in my career and continuously working toward new certifications, learning on the job, and refining my skills outside of work. Everything I learn gets documented here.

CyberFriday isn’t about pretending to know everything. It’s about building real skill over time — and documenting the climb.
