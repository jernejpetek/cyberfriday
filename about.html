About

Bio • Focus • Links.

Hi, I’m JP 👋

The creator of this website and the person behind CyberFriday. CyberFriday is where I document my hands-on work in cyber security through writeups, guides, and small projects.

My focus is learning by doing and sharing what I learn. I work through labs, CTFs, and practical exercises, then turn that experience into clear, structured writeups that document both the process and the lessons behind it.

In addition to hands-on work, I also publish personal notes and preparation material for certifications as part of my learning process, keeping everything organized and accessible in one place.

CyberFriday is built as a public knowledge base and a growing body of work. Everything here comes from real experimentation — from enumeration notes and exploitation paths to tools, projects, and certification prep developed along the way.

The goal is simple: build skill through practice, document what works, and share the journey as CyberFriday continues to grow.
