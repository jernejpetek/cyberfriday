About.

The story behind CyberFriday.

Hi, I'm JP.

I created CyberFriday to document my cybersecurity learning through hands-on labs, security writeups, certification study, and practical projects.

Here you'll find notes about tools and techniques, TryHackMe exercises, beginner-friendly experiments, and research that helps me deepen my understanding of offensive and defensive security.

Purpose

CyberFriday is a personal learning archive, shared openly for students and aspiring cybersecurity professionals. The focus is on clear documentation, ethical practice, and continuous improvement.

Ethical use

All content is intended for education and authorized security testing only. Always obtain permission before testing systems.
